PressPrep privacy policy

PressPrep: WP Dev Quiz · Effective 22 November 2026

The short version
  • There's no account. Your progress stays on your phone and in your own Android backup.
  • PressPrep doesn't use analytics and never sells personal data.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which collects some device data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads can be personalised, and you can change that at any time.
  • Tips are paid through Google Play. PressPrep never sees your card details.
  • If you report an error or contribute a question, you send only what you type into the form.

1. Who is responsible

PressPrep is an independent project created by Cesar Cruz ("I", "me"). I'm responsible for the personal data described here. You can contact me about privacy at pressprep.ta@gmail.com.

PressPrep isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the WordPress Foundation, WordPress.org or Automattic.

2. Data that stays on your device

PressPrep stores the following only on your phone:

  • your level, stage results, scores, rating and streak
  • your mistakes list and settings (such as reminder time and dark mode)
  • a count of the tips you've given, used only for the thank-you message

This data isn't sent to me. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may copy it to your own Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account; I can't access it.

You can export your progress to a file, import it again, or delete it with Profile › Your data › Reset progress. Uninstalling the app also deletes it from the phone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PressPrep is free and shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. According to Google, its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its ad partners may use this data to show and measure ads.

  • Your choice in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads load, you'll see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept personalised ads, or refuse them and see non-personalised or limited ads instead. You can change your choice at any time in Profile › Privacy and ad choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Supporters: after the supporter tip, PressPrep stops loading ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Tips and purchases (Google Play)

Tips are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google handles your payment details; I never receive them. The app receives a confirmation from Google Play (which product was bought and whether the purchase is valid) so it can turn off ads and restore supporter status on your other devices.

5. Content updates (GitHub Pages)

To get new questions without an app update, PressPrep downloads a small file from cesar-cruz-techadv.github.io, hosted by GitHub. The app doesn't send any personal data in these requests. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the files are downloaded. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Error reports and contributions (Tally)

If you use Report error or Contribute, the app opens a form in your browser, hosted by Tally (based in Belgium, with form data stored in Europe). The form contains only what you see before you submit it:

  • for error reports: the question ID, the app version, the reason you choose and any note you write
  • for contributions: your question or scenario, and optionally your name (for credit) and email (so I can reply)

I use this only to fix questions, review contributions and contact you if you asked me to. Accepted contributions are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence, with your name only if you chose to be credited. I delete form responses once they're resolved, and at the latest after 12 months. See Tally's privacy policy.

If you contribute through GitHub instead, what you post there is public and covered by GitHub's terms.

7. Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the reminder is scheduled on your phone. There's no notification server, and no data leaves your device for this.

8. What PressPrep doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs beyond Google AdMob as described above.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to data portability. Most PressPrep data never reaches me, so you control it directly on your device. For anything I hold, such as form responses, email pressprep.ta@gmail.com and I'll reply within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

For data held by Google or GitHub, use their privacy tools, linked above.

11. Children

PressPrep is intended for adults (18 and over) preparing for developer interviews and certifications. It isn't directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
